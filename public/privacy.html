RubberDeck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22 · Developer: AtaCanYmc

1. Data Collection

RubberDeck collects zero personal data, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or advertising identifiers. The application operates fully offline and makes no outbound network requests to developer or third-party servers.

2. Bluetooth and USB Hardware Access

RubberDeck requests Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) or USB Serial access solely to discover and send HID control frames to local RubberOtter and RubberOrca hardware peripherals using GATT service 0xFFE0 and characteristic 0xFFE1. Bluetooth scans are never used to infer or track physical location.

3. Local Storage

UI preferences (selected device profile, trackpad sensitivity, and haptics toggle) remain strictly in local device memory or storage and never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Links

RubberDeck includes optional external links to the open-source GitHub repositories and Web Serial firmware flashers for RubberOtter and RubberOrca. Opening those links leaves the application and is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or security reports, contact atacanymc@gmail.com or open a private advisory at github.com/AtaCanYmc/RubberDeck/security/policy .
